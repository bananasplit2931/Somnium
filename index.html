SOMNIUM

Welcome to the Somnium Project!

Somnium (Latin for dream) is an event and a mod created for the community, by the community. We want everyone to have fun and be happy, because not everyone knows how to code their own mod. Here, anyone can create a fish, a sprite, or an idea, and we will do our best to add it to the game!

Contest Rules & Guidelines

1. Art Style & Adjustments

We want your fish to look good and somewhat fit the "Cat Goes Fishing" style. If a fish heavily deviates from this style, our team will carefully redesign it, we will always try to preserve the creator's original vision!

2. How to Submit

Anyone who wants to participate must post their creation in our specific Discord channel. You MUST add the hashtag #Somnium (or other clear indicators, though the hashtag is preferred) to your message. This tells us the fish belongs to the project and gives us permission to use it.

Join the Discord Channel Cat Goes Fishing Server

3. Acceptance & Limitations

There is never a 100% guarantee that a specific fish will make it into the mod. We will create a "rejected fish" channel on our server with detailed reasons for any rejections. Submissions with extremely heavy features or things beyond our coding capabilities might be rejected, though we will always try to collaborate with other creators to make it work first.

Everyone is a Creator

Everyone who participates will be fully credited on this website and in the game's credits. We want to make it clear that this isn't just "our" mod, every participant is actively creating this mod with us until the project is finished or discontinued. Thank you for dreaming with us!
